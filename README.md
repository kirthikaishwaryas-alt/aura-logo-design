# Aura Logo Design Project (Task 2)

A professional, minimalist brand identity project developed for the **Aura** fashion and lifestyle brand, submitted as part of Task 2 via the Thiranex portal.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
The **Aura** brand identity project focuses on creating a modern, clean, and versatile aesthetic tailored for the contemporary fashion and lifestyle market. The core objective is to deliver a sophisticated visual mark that translates seamlessly across digital platforms, packaging, and physical apparel.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Concept
* **Minimalism:** Stripping away unnecessary complexity to focus on timeless elegance and clean lines.
* **Versatility:** Ensuring the logo maintains high legibility and impact across various scales, from small embroidered tags to large digital banners.
* **Aesthetic Balance:** Aligning typography and graphic balance to evoke a sense of modern luxury and style.

---

## 📐 Technical Specifications
* **Canvas Resolution:** Optimized for high-definition digital display (1080 × 1080 px).
* **Color Harmony:** Designed to function effectively across primary palette arrangements as well as high-contrast monochrome applications.
